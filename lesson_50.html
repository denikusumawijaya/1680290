Pemanasan: Ulasan Spiral

Spiral Review Warm-up
Tujuan: membaca pecahan dari gambar, membuat pecahan senilai, dan membandingkan pecahan berpenyebut sama.Goal: read a fraction from a picture, make equivalent fractions, and compare fractions with the same denominator.

A. Apa itu pecahan?A. What is a fraction?

Pecahan menunjukkan bagian dari keseluruhan yang dibagi menjadi bagian-bagian sama besar.A fraction shows a part of a whole that has been split into equal parts.

Rumus / Formula
\[\frac{a}{b} = \frac{\text{pembilang (bagian yang diambil)}}{\text{penyebut (banyak bagian sama besar)}}\]

numerator (parts taken) over denominator (number of equal parts)

3 dari 10 bagian diwarnai → \(\frac{3}{10}\)3 of 10 parts shaded
5 dari 10 bagian → \(\frac{5}{10}\)5 of 10 parts

\(\frac{3}{10}\) dibaca "tiga per sepuluh". Bagian yang tidak diwarnai adalah \(\frac{10-3}{10} = \frac{7}{10}\).\(\frac{3}{10}\) is read "three tenths". The part that is not shaded is \(\frac{7}{10}\).

B. Pecahan senilaiB. Equivalent fractions

Pecahan senilai adalah pecahan yang nilainya sama walaupun angkanya berbeda. Caranya: kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan bilangan yang sama.Equivalent fractions have the same value even though the numbers differ. Multiply the numerator and the denominator by the same number.

Rumus / Formula
\[\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \times n}{b \times n}, \quad n \neq 0\]
Contoh 1 / Example 1

Isilah: \(\frac{2}{5} = \frac{\square}{10}\)Fill in the box.

  1. Cari pengali penyebut: \(5 \times \square = 10\), jadi pengalinya \(10 \div 5 = 2\).Find what the denominator was multiplied by: \(10 \div 5 = 2\).
  2. Kalikan pembilang dengan pengali yang sama: \(2 \times 2 = 4\).Multiply the numerator by the same number: \(2 \times 2 = 4\).
  3. \(\frac{2}{5} = \frac{2 \times 2}{5 \times 2} = \frac{4}{10}\)
Contoh 2 / Example 2

Apakah \(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{50}{100}\)?Is \(\frac{1}{2}\) equal to \(\frac{50}{100}\)?

  1. Pengali penyebut: \(100 \div 2 = 50\).Denominator multiplier: \(100 \div 2 = 50\).
  2. Pembilang: \(1 \times 50 = 50\). Hasilnya sama dengan 50.Numerator: \(1 \times 50 = 50\). It matches.
  3. Jadi pernyataan itu benar: \(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{50}{100}\).So the statement is true.
Cara cepat / Shortcut

Pecahan senilai \(\frac{1}{2}\) selalu punya penyebut = \(2 \times\) pembilang. Contoh: \(\frac{3}{6}\) karena \(3 \times 2 = 6\) ✔. Tetapi \(\frac{3}{5}\) bukan, karena \(3 \times 2 = 6 \neq 5\).A fraction equal to one half always has denominator = 2 × numerator. \(\frac{3}{6}\) works; \(\frac{3}{5}\) does not.

C. Membandingkan pecahan berpenyebut samaC. Comparing fractions with the same denominator

Jika penyebutnya sama, bandingkan pembilangnya. Pembilang lebih besar berarti pecahan lebih besar.If the denominators are the same, compare the numerators. The bigger numerator gives the bigger fraction.

Contoh 3 / Example 3

Urutkan dari terkecil: \(\frac{8}{10}, \frac{2}{10}, \frac{5}{10}\)Order from smallest.

  1. Penyebut semua 10, jadi lihat pembilang: 8, 2, 5.All denominators are 10, so look at the numerators: 8, 2, 5.
  2. Urutkan pembilang: \(2 \lt 5 \lt 8\).Order the numerators.
  3. Hasil: \(\frac{2}{10} \lt \frac{5}{10} \lt \frac{8}{10}\)

Pendahuluan & Koneksi Dunia Nyata

Introduction & Real-World Connection
Tujuan: memahami arti persen dan menemukannya dalam kehidupan sehari-hari.Goal: understand what percent means and find it in everyday life.

Persen = "per seratus"Percent = "per hundred"

Kata persen berasal dari bahasa Latin per centum, artinya "dari setiap seratus". Simbolnya \(\%\). Jadi persen selalu membandingkan bagian dengan keseluruhan yang dianggap 100.The word percent comes from the Latin per centum, meaning "out of every hundred". Its symbol is %. A percent always compares a part with a whole counted as 100.

Rumus / Formula
\[a\% = \frac{a}{100}\]

\(a\) bagian dari 100 bagian sama besar.a parts out of 100 equal parts.

\(1\% = \frac{1}{100}\)1 square of 100
\(25\% = \frac{25}{100}\)one quarter
\(50\% = \frac{50}{100}\)one half
\(100\% = \frac{100}{100} = 1\)the whole

Persen di sekitar kitaPercent around us

Baterai HP \(75\%\): terisi 75 dari 100 bagian.Phone battery 75%: 75 of 100 parts charged.
Diskon \(50\%\): harga dipotong setengahnya.50% off: the price is cut in half.
Peluang hujan \(30\%\): 30 dari 100 kemungkinan.30% chance of rain: 30 out of 100.
Nilai 80 dari 100 soal = \(80\%\) benar.80 of 100 questions correct = 80%.

Cara menulis persenHow to write a percent

Contoh 1 / Example 1

Di aula ada 100 kursi. 60 kursi terisi. Berapa persen kursi terisi? Berapa persen kosong?A hall has 100 chairs. 60 are taken. What percent are taken? What percent are empty?

hijau = terisigreen = taken
  1. Pastikan keseluruhannya 100: ada 100 kursi ✔.Check that the whole is 100: there are 100 chairs.
  2. Bagian yang terisi = 60, tulis \(\frac{60}{100}\).The taken part is 60, write \(\frac{60}{100}\).
  3. Ubah ke persen: \(\frac{60}{100} = 60\%\).Change to percent.
  4. Keseluruhan selalu \(100\%\), jadi kosong \(= 100\% - 60\% = 40\%\).The whole is always 100%, so empty = 100% − 60% = 40%.
Patokan penting / Key benchmarks
  • \(100\%\) = seluruhnya / all of it · \(50\%\) = setengah / half · \(25\%\) = seperempat / a quarter · \(10\%\) = 10 dari 100 / 10 out of 100 · \(0\%\) = tidak ada / none
  • Diskon \(100\%\) artinya seluruh harga dipotong, jadi barang gratis.100% off means the whole price is removed, so the item is free.
  • Kurang dari \(50\%\) = kurang dari setengah. Lebih dari \(50\%\) = lebih dari setengah.Less than 50% is less than half; more than 50% is more than half.

Mengulas Pengetahuan Sebelumnya

Review of Prior Knowledge
Tujuan: mengingat nilai tempat desimal (persepuluhan & perseratusan) dan mengubah pecahan perseratus ke desimal.Goal: recall decimal place value (tenths & hundredths) and change hundredths fractions into decimals.

Nilai tempat desimalDecimal place value

Di Indonesia, bilangan desimal ditulis dengan koma. Angka di kanan koma adalah bagian dari satu.In Indonesia decimals are written with a comma. The digits to the right of the comma are parts of one.

BilanganNumberSatuanOnes,PersepuluhanTenthsPerseratusanHundredthsPecahanFraction
\(0{,}1\)0,1–\(\frac{1}{10}\)
\(0{,}01\)0,01\(\frac{1}{100}\)
\(0{,}68\)0,68\(\frac{68}{100}\)

\(0{,}68 = 0{,}6 + 0{,}08\): angka 6 bernilai 6 persepuluhan, angka 8 bernilai 8 perseratusan.In 0,68 the digit 6 is worth 6 tenths and the digit 8 is worth 8 hundredths.

\(0{,}3 = \frac{3}{10} = \frac{30}{100}\)3 whole rows
\(0{,}03 = \frac{3}{100}\)only 3 small squares

Pecahan perseratus ↔ desimalHundredths fraction ↔ decimal

Aturan / Rule

Penyebut 10 → 1 angka di belakang koma. Penyebut 100 → 2 angka di belakang koma.Denominator 10 → 1 digit after the comma. Denominator 100 → 2 digits after the comma.

\[\frac{45}{100} = 0{,}45 \qquad \frac{5}{100} = 0{,}05 \qquad \frac{3}{10} = 0{,}3\]
Contoh 1 / Example 1 · pecahan → desimal

Ubah \(\frac{5}{100}\) ke desimal.Change \(\frac{5}{100}\) to a decimal.

  1. Penyebutnya 100, jadi harus ada 2 angka di belakang koma.The denominator is 100, so we need 2 digits after the comma.
  2. Pembilang 5 hanya 1 angka, maka tambahkan 0 di depannya sebagai penjaga tempat: 05.The numerator 5 has only 1 digit, so put a 0 in front as a placeholder: 05.
  3. \(\frac{5}{100} = 0{,}05\) (bukan \(0{,}5\)!)not 0,5!
Contoh 2 / Example 2 · desimal → pecahan

Ubah \(0{,}45\) ke pecahan.Change 0,45 to a fraction.

  1. Ada 2 angka di belakang koma → penyebutnya 100.2 digits after the comma → denominator 100.
  2. Angka di belakang koma (45) menjadi pembilang.The digits after the comma (45) become the numerator.
  3. \(0{,}45 = \frac{45}{100}\)
Contoh 3 / Example 3 · menambah nol

Apakah \(0{,}5 = 0{,}50\)?Is 0,5 equal to 0,50?

  1. \(0{,}5 = \frac{5}{10}\). Kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan 10: \(\frac{5 \times 10}{10 \times 10} = \frac{50}{100}\).Multiply top and bottom by 10.
  2. \(\frac{50}{100} = 0{,}50\). Jadi \(0{,}5 = 0{,}50\) benar. Menambah nol di ujung kanan desimal tidak mengubah nilai.So it is true. Adding a zero at the right end of a decimal does not change its value.

Pengenalan Masalah

Problem Introduction
Tujuan: melihat bahwa pecahan perseratus, desimal perseratusan, dan persen adalah tiga cara menulis nilai yang sama.Goal: see that a hundredths fraction, a hundredths decimal and a percent are three ways to write the same value.

Masalah: Kebun sekolahProblem: The school garden

Kebun sekolah dibagi menjadi 100 petak sama besar. Sebanyak 35 petak ditanami bayam. Nyatakan bagian yang ditanami bayam dalam bentuk pecahan, desimal, dan persen.The school garden is split into 100 equal plots. 35 plots are planted with spinach. Write the spinach part as a fraction, a decimal and a percent.

35 petak hijau dari 10035 green plots out of 100
  1. Hitung bagian yang diwarnai: 35. Keseluruhan: 100.Count the shaded part: 35. The whole: 100.
  2. Pecahan: \(\frac{35}{100}\)Fraction.
  3. Desimal (penyebut 100 → 2 angka di belakang koma): \(0{,}35\)Decimal (denominator 100 → 2 digits after the comma).
  4. Persen (per seratus → pembilangnya diberi \(\%\)): \(35\%\)Percent (per hundred → put % after the numerator).
Rumus kunci / Key formula
\[\frac{35}{100} = 0{,}35 = 35\%\] \[\text{desimal} \times 100 = \text{persen} \qquad \text{persen} \div 100 = \text{desimal}\]

decimal × 100 = percent; percent ÷ 100 = decimal

Tiga arah perubahanThree directions of change

Contoh 1 / Example 1 · persen → pecahan → desimal

Ubah \(8\%\).Change 8%.

  1. \(8\% = \frac{8}{100}\) (persen artinya per seratus).Percent means per hundred.
  2. Penyebut 100 → 2 angka di belakang koma, tambah 0 penjaga tempat: \(\frac{8}{100} = 0{,}08\).Denominator 100 → 2 digits, add a placeholder 0.
Contoh 2 / Example 2 · desimal → persen

Ubah \(0{,}7\) ke persen.Change 0,7 to a percent.

  1. Jadikan 2 angka di belakang koma: \(0{,}7 = 0{,}70\).Make it 2 digits after the comma.
  2. \(0{,}70 = \frac{70}{100} = 70\%\). Atau: \(0{,}7 \times 100 = 70\), jadi \(70\%\).Or multiply by 100: 0,7 × 100 = 70.
Hati-hati / Careful

\(0{,}7\) bukan \(7\%\). \(7\% = 0{,}07\).0,7 is not 7%. 7% = 0,07.

Contoh 3 / Example 3 · membandingkan bentuk campuran

Urutkan dari terkecil: \(0{,}4;\ 15\%;\ \frac{3}{100};\ 0{,}72\)Order from smallest.

  1. Ubah semua ke persen: \(0{,}4 = 0{,}40 = 40\%\), \(15\%\), \(\frac{3}{100} = 3\%\), \(0{,}72 = 72\%\).Change everything to percent.
  2. Urutkan angkanya: \(3 \lt 15 \lt 40 \lt 72\).Order the numbers.
  3. Hasil: \(\frac{3}{100};\ 15\%;\ 0{,}4;\ 0{,}72\)
Contoh 4 / Example 4 · mencari bentuk yang senilai

Mana yang sama dengan \(30\%\)?Which ones equal 30%?

  1. \(30\% = \frac{30}{100} = 0{,}30 = 0{,}3\), dan \(\frac{30}{100} = \frac{3}{10}\).These are all the same value.
  2. \(0{,}03\) bukan, karena \(0{,}03 = 3\%\).0,03 is not, because 0,03 = 3%.

Latihan Terbimbing

Guided Practice
Tujuan: mengubah pecahan dengan penyebut 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, atau 50 menjadi perseratus, desimal, dan persen.Goal: change fractions with denominators 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25 or 50 into hundredths, decimals and percents.

Jadikan penyebutnya 100Make the denominator 100

Persen selalu "per seratus". Jadi jika penyebut belum 100, kita ubah dulu menjadi 100 memakai pecahan senilai.Percent is always "per hundred". If the denominator is not 100 yet, change it to 100 using equivalent fractions.

Rumus / Formula
\[n = 100 \div b \qquad \frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \times n}{b \times n} = \frac{\square}{100} = \square\%\]
Penyebut \(b\)Denominator24510202550
Pengali \(n = 100 \div b\)Multiplier\(\times 50\)\(\times 25\)\(\times 20\)\(\times 10\)\(\times 5\)\(\times 4\)\(\times 2\)

Contoh langkah demi langkahStep-by-step examples

Contoh A / Example A

Ubah \(\frac{3}{4}\) ke perseratus, desimal, dan persen.Change 3/4 to hundredths, a decimal and a percent.

\(\frac{3}{4}\) = 75 kotak75 squares
  1. Cari pengali: \(100 \div 4 = 25\).Find the multiplier.
  2. Kalikan atas dan bawah: \(\frac{3 \times 25}{4 \times 25} = \frac{75}{100}\).Multiply top and bottom.
  3. Desimal: \(\frac{75}{100} = 0{,}75\). Persen: \(75\%\).Decimal and percent.
Contoh B / Example B

Ubah \(\frac{2}{5}\).Change 2/5.

  1. \(100 \div 5 = 20\)
  2. \(\frac{2 \times 20}{5 \times 20} = \frac{40}{100} = 0{,}40 = 40\%\)
Contoh C / Example C

Ubah \(\frac{7}{20}\).Change 7/20.

  1. \(100 \div 20 = 5\)
  2. \(\frac{7 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{35}{100} = 0{,}35 = 35\%\)
Contoh D / Example D

Benarkah \(\frac{9}{25} = 36\%\)?Is 9/25 = 36%?

  1. \(100 \div 25 = 4\), lalu \(\frac{9 \times 4}{25 \times 4} = \frac{36}{100} = 36\%\). Jadi benar.So it is true.
Pecahan patokan untuk dihafal / Benchmarks to remember
\[\frac{1}{2} = 0{,}5 = 50\% \quad \frac{1}{4} = 0{,}25 = 25\% \quad \frac{3}{4} = 0{,}75 = 75\% \quad \frac{1}{5} = 0{,}2 = 20\% \quad \frac{1}{10} = 0{,}1 = 10\%\]

Latihan Mandiri

Independent Practice
Tujuan: menyelesaikan soal cerita dan membandingkan nilai dalam bentuk pecahan, desimal, dan persen.Goal: solve word problems and compare values given as fractions, decimals and percents.

Langkah menyelesaikan soal ceritaSteps for word problems

  1. Baca soal. Temukan bagian dan keseluruhan.Read the problem. Find the part and the whole.
  2. Tulis pecahannya: \(\dfrac{\text{bagian}}{\text{keseluruhan}}\)Write the fraction: part over whole.
  3. Ubah penyebut menjadi 100 (kalikan dengan \(n = 100 \div \text{penyebut}\)).Change the denominator to 100.
  4. Tulis dalam desimal atau persen sesuai pertanyaan.Write it as a decimal or percent as asked.
  5. Jawab dengan kalimat lengkap.Answer in a full sentence.
Contoh 1 / Example 1

Di kelas ada 20 siswa. 7 siswa suka sepak bola. Berapa persen siswa yang suka sepak bola?A class has 20 students. 7 like football. What percent like football?

  1. Bagian = 7, keseluruhan = 20 → \(\frac{7}{20}\).Part = 7, whole = 20.
  2. \(100 \div 20 = 5\) → \(\frac{7 \times 5}{20 \times 5} = \frac{35}{100}\)
  3. \(\frac{35}{100} = 35\%\). Jadi \(35\%\) siswa suka sepak bola.So 35% of the students like football.
Contoh 2 / Example 2 · membandingkan

Diskon Toko A \(\frac{1}{4}\), diskon Toko B \(30\%\). Diskon mana yang lebih besar?Shop A gives 1/4 off, shop B gives 30% off. Which discount is bigger?

  1. Ubah ke persen: \(\frac{1}{4} = \frac{25}{100} = 25\%\).Change to percent.
  2. Bandingkan: \(25\% \lt 30\%\). Jadi diskon Toko B lebih besar.So shop B's discount is bigger.
Contoh 3 / Example 3 · mengurutkan nilai

Nilai: Lina \(0{,}7\), Bayu \(\frac{3}{5}\), Sari \(65\%\). Urutkan dari terkecil.Scores: Lina 0,7, Bayu 3/5, Sari 65%. Order from smallest.

  1. Lina: \(0{,}7 = 0{,}70 = 70\%\). Bayu: \(\frac{3 \times 20}{5 \times 20} = \frac{60}{100} = 60\%\). Sari: \(65\%\).
  2. \(60 \lt 65 \lt 70\) → Bayu, Sari, Lina.
Contoh 4 / Example 4 · keseluruhan 25

Dari 25 buah jeruk, 8 buah busuk. Nyatakan dalam desimal dan persen.8 of 25 oranges are rotten. Write it as a decimal and a percent.

  1. \(\frac{8}{25}\), pengali \(100 \div 25 = 4\) → \(\frac{8 \times 4}{25 \times 4} = \frac{32}{100}\)
  2. \(\frac{32}{100} = 0{,}32 = 32\%\)

Konsolidasi & Refleksi

Consolidation & Reflection

Poin pentingKey points

  • Persen artinya per seratus: \(a\% = \frac{a}{100}\).Percent means per hundred.
  • Pecahan perseratus ditulis desimal dengan 2 angka di belakang koma: \(\frac{6}{100} = 0{,}06\), \(\frac{45}{100} = 0{,}45\).A hundredths fraction becomes a decimal with 2 digits after the comma.
  • Desimal → persen: kalikan 100. \(0{,}3 = 0{,}30 = 30\%\).Decimal → percent: multiply by 100.
  • Persen → desimal: bagi 100. \(8\% = 0{,}08\).Percent → decimal: divide by 100.
  • Pecahan lain: jadikan penyebut 100 dengan \(n = 100 \div b\), lalu \(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \times n}{b \times n}\).Other fractions: make the denominator 100 first.
  • Untuk membandingkan, ubah semua ke bentuk yang sama (misalnya persen).To compare, change everything into the same form (for example percent).
